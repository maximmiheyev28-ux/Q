# Game art credits

## Samurai player and Demon Samurai boss

Original pixel-art sprite sheets from the **FULL Samurai 2D Pixel Art v1.2** and **Demon Samurai 2D Pixel Art** packs. The pack license is included in `player_asset_license.txt` and `boss_asset_license.txt`.

The included license permits use in personal and commercial game projects and modification. It prohibits reselling or redistributing the files as a standalone game asset pack. The assets are used here as part of the NEON BLADE game project.

Source mirror containing the sprite sheets and the original license text:
https://github.com/grzeczko/samurai-greg/tree/main/frontend/assets/characters

## City stage and street enemies

**Streets of Fight** pixel-art pack by **Ansimuz**. The city stage, street punk and brawler sprite sheets are included as game artwork. Source:
https://opengameart.org/content/streets-of-fight

License: CC0 / public domain dedication as listed on the OpenGameArt asset page.

## Asset handling

All shipped game art is stored as PNG files under `assets/`. The Android build validates and packages these files directly. It does not draw or synthesize any character, enemy, background, weapon, projectile or effect artwork in Python.
